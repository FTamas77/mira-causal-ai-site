Same question.
Same data.
Different evidence.

What does a specialized causal AI tool actually add to ChatGPT? We tested the same synthetic cooling problem in two conversations: with the private mira connector and without it.

Both solved the supplied decision equations. Only the mira-enabled run executed PCMCI+ in the tested environment—and its reported 11 links matched the synthetic data-generating graph exactly.

05 / RESEARCH CONCLUSION

In this controlled, one-run synthetic demonstration, mira added verifiable research functionality: PCMCI+ execution and structured, inspectable discovery output where the ChatGPT-only sandbox could not execute the method. Its reported 11-link graph exactly matched the known 11-link generating structure. Both approaches computed the same correct explicit-SCM intervention decision. This is evidence of specialized tool utility in the tested environment—not proof of general superiority, real-world causal validity or automatic causal-effect estimation.

Pre-release evidence, private-hosted mira v0.4.0, 19 November 2026. Not a claim of OpenAI app approval. A new study with the publicly released app, additional datasets/seeds and repeat trials is planned after release.
