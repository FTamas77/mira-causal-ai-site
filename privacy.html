Current scope. This is a research-stage privacy notice, not a finalized data protection notice for a public commercial service. Any broader release requires a review of actual hosting, processors, retention and applicable legal obligations.

1. This website

This static GitHub Pages website does not ask visitors to register, submit forms or provide project data. The pages do not include project-operated analytics, advertising tags or tracking scripts. GitHub, as the website host, may process technical information about visits under its privacy statement.

2. Experimental causal computations

The mira backend processes client-supplied models, observations and assumptions for scientific calculations. The research implementation is stateless and does not intentionally persist submitted models, datasets or user sessions as application records. Hosting and connected platforms may still process technical operational data separately.

3. Third-party platforms

If you interact with the research through ChatGPT, GitHub or a hosting provider, those platforms may independently process information under their own policies and operational settings. Their retention and processing practices are not controlled by this static website.

4. Appropriate research data

Use synthetic, public or appropriately anonymized data only. Do not send personal, sensitive or confidential data, credentials, proprietary industrial datasets or material that you do not have permission to share.

5. Contact

Questions about this research website can be directed to the project contact page. Do not post private information to public GitHub issues.
