mira Causal AI

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

mira Causal AI is an independent, early-stage causal decision-support service operated by Tamas Fekete (Hungary). This notice covers the mira service and its public website. Contact the operator and data controller at fekete331@gmail.com.

Information processed and why

When you request a calculation, the connected AI platform sends the structured inputs needed for the task: causal graphs, model equations, variable values, assumptions, evidence references, candidate interventions, objectives and constraints. These inputs may contain personal data if you or the AI client include it. mira processes them to validate the model, compute results and return an auditable decision trace. The service does not require a separate user account or collect profile information for its calculations.

The hosted application records limited operational information, such as timestamps, HTTP method, redacted or allowlisted route, response status and security-check indicators, for service reliability, troubleshooting and abuse prevention. It is designed not to log request bodies or credentials. Hosting providers may separately process network and diagnostic metadata, including IP addresses.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. GitHub Issues are public; do not post personal or confidential information there. The project website does not include its own tracking scripts or advertising cookies.

Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, processing is based on the necessity of providing the service you request (Article 6(1)(b), where applicable), legitimate interests in operating and securing the service and responding to support requests (Article 6(1)(f)), or compliance with applicable legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)). We do not use model inputs for advertising, profiling, sale of personal data or training an AI model.

Recipients and international transfers

The AI platform (OpenAI) sends tool requests and receives results. Render hosts the computation service; GitHub hosts the website and public issue tracker; Google provides the support email service. These providers may process data needed to deliver their services under their applicable terms. Personal data may be processed outside the EEA, including in the United States. Render describes its transfer safeguards, including the Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses where applicable, in its Data Processing Addendum. Contact us for information about applicable safeguards.

See the providers' privacy information: OpenAI, Render, GitHub and Google. Their independent processing and retention may differ from mira's.

Retention

mira's causal engine processes models and calculation results in memory for the request and does not persist them or maintain user sessions. Results may remain in the AI client's conversation history. Render documents seven-day dashboard log retention for Hobby workspaces; other workspace plans and provider-side operational records may have different retention periods. See Render's logging documentation.

Routine support correspondence is to be deleted or anonymized within 90 days after the request is resolved, unless a longer period is necessary for an unresolved issue, legal obligation or legal claim. Public GitHub Issues remain visible until removed; GitHub's own retention rules also apply.

Your choices and rights

You can stop using the service, disconnect the plugin in your AI client, and choose not to include unnecessary or identifying information. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing. Email the controller at fekete331@gmail.com. We will respond within the applicable statutory time limits. Requests relating to data held separately by a provider may need to be addressed to that provider.

You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority or Hungary's National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH).

Data minimization and children

Use anonymized or synthetic inputs whenever possible. Do not submit health records, special-category personal data, government identifiers, payment card details, passwords, access tokens, API keys, personal data about children or confidential third-party data. mira is not directed at children under 13. The backend provides decision support and does not autonomously take decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on individuals.

We may update this notice when the service or its data practices change.
