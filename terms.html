Research and education. mira Causal AI is an experimental causal-analysis research project, not a validated operational or professional decision-making service.

1. Research tools

The project provides scientific explanations and, where available, tools for validating directed acyclic graphs, exploring continuous time-series relationships with PCMCI+/analytic ParCorr and explicit background knowledge, and comparing interventions on separately specified structural causal models (SCMs). The website includes synthetic demonstration examples. The backend does not offer account registration, paid subscriptions, independent data acquisition or external actions.

2. Scientific limitations

Results depend on the data, assumptions, model structure and objective supplied for each request. Discovered statistical links do not establish causal truth; partial correlations are test statistics rather than identified causal effects. The current system does not automatically fit structural equations from discovered graphs or provide general counterfactual inference or causal-effect confidence intervals.

Model-based decisions are valid only for the specified model and inputs. Synthetic results do not constitute validated operational evidence. Review the assumptions, provenance, uncertainty and calculation traces independently.

3. Appropriate use and information

Use mira for experimental research, education and technical evaluation, not as a sole basis for medical, legal, financial, regulatory, safety-critical or other high-stakes decisions. The prototype does not execute real-world interventions.

Provide only material you have permission to share. Prefer synthetic or appropriately anonymized research data. Do not submit sensitive personal details, medical records, payment card information, government identifiers, credentials or confidential business datasets. Do not attempt to circumvent limits, interfere with availability or abuse the service.
