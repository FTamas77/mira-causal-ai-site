Experimental status. mira is currently a research prototype. It is not offered as a validated operational decision service. Final public application terms, if needed, will be established before broader release.

1. Scientific and educational use

The project explores time-series causal discovery, knowledge-constrained analysis and decisions evaluated using explicit structural models. Demonstrations and computed examples are for research and education.

2. Results and limitations

Results depend on supplied data, model structure, assumptions and numerical methods. Causal-discovery output does not establish causal truth, estimate identified intervention effects or automatically generate a valid structural causal model. Independently verify all interpretations and results.

3. Not professional advice

Do not rely on the prototype alone for operational, medical, legal, financial, regulatory or safety-critical decisions. It does not act on external systems or execute real-world interventions.

4. Responsible data use

Use information you have permission to provide. Avoid personal, sensitive, confidential or proprietary data and never submit credentials. Public GitHub discussions should contain only non-sensitive information.

5. Availability and affiliations

The project and its ChatGPT integration are experimental and may change or become unavailable. mira Causal AI is a research initiative, not an official OpenAI service. The researcher's academic and professional experience is included as background information and does not imply sponsorship by any named institution or company.

6. Legal status

The research materials are provided as is, without warranty to the extent permitted by law. Nothing here excludes liability or statutory rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. These research-stage notices are not a substitute for finalized applicable service terms.
