Evidence support is not causal probability

The versioned policy causal-support-ordinal-v1 assigns ordinal levels and explains its rules. Validation, audit and statistical support remain separate.

L0 · Unknown

No usable support for an unknown or invalidated claim.

L1 · Hypothesized

Explicit proposal, assumption or LLM hypothesis.

L2 · Supported

Active dated material evidence, as reported.

L3 · Corroborated

Successful registered independent synthetic replicate procedure.

L4 · Validated

Successful registered synthetic SCM validation within scope.

Dependencies cap support at the weakest prerequisite. An active contradiction or contested prerequisite caps support at L1. Duplicate and dependent evidence cannot establish independence. The policy retains earlier evidence and assessments.

In the experiment, the audited assumption remained L1 even after the calculation audit passed. Both the audit and validation were internal synthetic procedures, not independent external reviews. Reported statistical significance does not establish causality.

Provenance and rule trace

The original heat_analysis statistical result (r = 0.70, p = 0.003, n = 200) remains active alongside new_heat_counter, a synthetic contradictory observation addressing relationship existence. R1 and R2 recognize the proposal and reported material evidence; CONTESTED caps its support. DEPENDENCIES propagates contestation to the dependent LLM hypothesis. Both revised assessments appear at revision 9; the original assessments remain at revisions 2 and 3.

All evidence is synthetic, with source references under fixture:cooling/. The registered replicate inputs have distinct hashes; the duplicate evidence declares its dependency. The validation and audit procedures reproduced expected temperature pairs [38, 36] and [36, 34] in the supplied synthetic model.

Research outlook

Evidence support is a foundation for uncertainty-aware causal investigation, not a substitute for identification or uncertainty quantification.

These results come from author-provided live ChatGPT tool reports, not independent raw-response replay. The graph is not an identified SCM; the synthetic validation does not establish real-world effects. Alternative causal structures, parameter uncertainty, sensitivity analysis and robust intervention decisions remain research directions. Live multi-user isolation and production durability also remain to be validated.
